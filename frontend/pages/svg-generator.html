Convert images to SVG.

Turn raster images (PNG, JPG, WebP) into clean, scalable SVG path graphics. Adjust the threshold to control how much detail is preserved, and pick colors for the background and foreground paths.

Lower values treat more pixels as foreground (more detail). Default 128 is a good starting point.
